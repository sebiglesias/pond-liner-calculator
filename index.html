Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard formula for calculating pond liner size?
The industry standard formula is: **Liner Length = Pond Length + (2 × Maximum Depth) + (2 × Overlap Collar)** **Liner Width = Pond Width + (2 × Maximum Depth) + (2 × Overlap Collar)** Always include at least 1.5 feet of overlap on every side to anchor the liner beneath heavy perimeter perimeter boulders.
Why is 45 Mil EPDM rubber preferred over PVC or HDPE for fish ponds?
45 mil EPDM rubber contains zero toxic plasticizers that leach into water and harm Koi fish or plants. It is highly elastic (stretches over 300% to conform to shelf contours) and has a 25+ year lifespan resistant to sunlight and freeze-thaw cycles. PVC becomes brittle and cracks in sub-freezing winters.
How deep must a backyard Koi pond be in cold winter climates?
In northern climates that experience hard ground freezes, a Koi pond must be at least 3.0 to 4.0 feet deep. The top 6 to 12 inches may freeze into solid ice, but the water beneath will remain an insulated 39°F (4°C), where fish can safely hibernate.
